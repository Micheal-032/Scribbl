Forgot password

Scribbl stores secure note keys derived from your password locally. If you have secure notes and you lose your password, they cannot be decrypted. Options:

  • Try other passwords you might have used.
  • If you previously exported a backup (Settings → Export), restore from the backup.
  • To reset the local account and start fresh, you can clear local data — this will delete all notes.
